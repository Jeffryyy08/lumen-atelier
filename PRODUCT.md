# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Motion for React, and a modular component structure.

## Users

People looking for a high-end beauty salon who want to understand the point of view, see the work, and request an appointment from a mobile or desktop browser.

## Product Purpose

The site introduces a boutique salon, communicates its service philosophy, and makes appointment intent easy to act on. Success means a first-time visitor understands the experience and can reach booking without friction.

## Positioning

The demo is positioned around an editorial, sensory presentation of beauty services instead of a generic service directory. This is a design demonstration, not a live booking system.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from social profiles, referrals, or search and scan quickly. They may be on a phone while deciding whether the salon feels worth a visit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One responsive landing page with anchor navigation.
- Service discovery, studio point of view, selected work, testimonials, and appointment CTA.
- Appointment interaction is demonstrative and should be clearly treated as a front-end demo.
- Images may use remote Unsplash URLs; content is illustrative and must not imply verified claims.

## Brand Commitments

No existing brand assets were supplied. The working demo identity is named LUMEN Atelier, uses Spanish copy, and is localized to San José, Costa Rica.

## Evidence on Hand

No real testimonials, staff bios, address, pricing, or booking backend were supplied. The San José location, Costa Rican colón prices, schedule, and social handle in the demo are illustrative and labeled accordingly.

## Product Principles

- Make the salon's point of view legible before listing services.
- Let imagery and whitespace carry the feeling of the visit.
- Keep appointment intent visible without turning the page into a sales funnel.
- Make the experience calm, responsive, and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, readable copy, and touch targets sized for mobile use.
