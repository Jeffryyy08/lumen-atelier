# LUMEN Atelier — Design Direction

## World

Cold luxury with human warmth: a quiet, gallery-like salon site built from ink black, pale mineral, soft smoke, and one restrained coral accent. The visual language borrows from contemporary beauty editorials and material studies rather than spa clichés.

## Dials

- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 8
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 6
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 4

## Typography

Use Geist Sans for the interface and a restrained italic treatment of the same family for emphasis. Avoid mixed-family display gimmicks. Large text is compact, left-aligned, and allowed to breathe.

## Palette

- Ink: #101010
- Mineral: #f2f0ec
- Smoke: #d9d5cf
- Coral accent: #d97d6d
- Muted ink: #6b6964

## Shape and depth

Cards and media use a consistent 16px radius. Buttons are pill-shaped controls. Elevation is sparse: prefer borders and whitespace, with a single soft, tinted shadow on the hero media stack.

## First viewport

Split composition: editorial statement and booking action on the left, layered portrait/editorial image treatment on the right. The hero should make the salon feel specific even before the visitor scrolls.

## Signature interaction

The hero image stack responds to pointer position with a restrained 3D tilt and a moving highlight; it falls back to a static composition on touch and reduced-motion preferences.

## Motion

Use one orchestrated entrance sequence, gentle section reveals, and tactile hover/press states. No infinite ornamental animation.

## Content tone

Spanish, direct, sensory, and self-assured. Avoid generic wellness promises and fabricated social proof. Demo-only operational details are explicitly labeled in the UI or surrounding copy.
